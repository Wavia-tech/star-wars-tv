# Prompts des décors

Principe : chaque décor reprend la **composition, la lumière et l'émotion** d'une scène culte, avec des designs à nous
(architecture, costumes, véhicules). Les fans reconnaissent le moment ; l'image ne recopie aucun élément protégé.
La fidélité aux films passe aussi par ce que l'appli ajoute elle-même : les éclairs, les sabres aux couleurs de chaque
épisode, les sons.

Réglages : 16:9, 2K, mode illimité. Le bas de l'image (un cinquième) reste calme : les logos s'y posent.

## Côté lumineux : le coucher des deux soleils

```text
Ultra-detailed cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9. Dusk on a remote desert farming world with two suns setting
side by side just above a flat horizon: a larger pale yellow-white sun and a smaller deep orange sun close to its right,
two distinct glowing discs, a wide amber sky fading to dusty violet. On the left third, seen from behind, a young farmhand
in a loose undyed linen tunic and wrapped boots stands still on a low sand ridge, looking at the suns, his long shadow
running back toward the camera. Around him a modest homestead: slender weathered metal condenser masts with small dish
caps planted in the sand, a low rounded sandstone dome half sunk into the ground on the right, an old open-top hover
skiff parked beside it with patched panels. Far on the horizon, a tiny silhouette of a huge tracked salvage vehicle.
Rippled sand, heat shimmer, warm rim light, quiet longing, 1970s anamorphic film look, fine grain. No text, no watermark.
Bottom fifth of the frame calm sand in soft shadow.
```

## Côté obscur : la salle du trône

```text
Ultra-detailed cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9. The private audience chamber of an ancient tyrant at the
summit of an immense orbital fortress. Camera low, behind a tall angular obsidian throne in the lower centre: over its
edge the cowled head of an old robed ruler seen from behind, one withered hand raised to the side. Before him a vast
panoramic viewport of segmented dark glass panes in a heavy geometric frame fills the upper two thirds of the frame;
beyond it a lush green moon and a distant fleet battle with flashes of orange light and drifting debris. A wide stair of
black metal with thin cold light strips leads up to the throne. Two ceremonial sentinels in long deep-red cloaks and
smooth faceless red helms stand perfectly still at either side of the stair, holding tall staves. Mirror-black floor
reflecting the viewport, cold steel-blue light, deep shadow, faint haze, 1980s practical-set look, anamorphic, fine
grain. No text, no watermark. Bottom fifth of the frame dark and calm.
```

## La salle des sabres

```text
Ultra-detailed cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9. A vast hall in an ancient monastery of warrior-guardians, at
night. Straight-on symmetrical view down a nave of towering ribbed columns in pale sand-coloured stone, with tall slit
windows letting in cold blue moonlight beams full of floating dust. At the far end a huge arched opening onto an endless
night city of luminous towers and slow rivers of flying traffic. Across the lower middle of the frame, at about 70 percent
of the image height, a long low ledge of dark polished stone runs the full width of the image, completely empty. Polished
floor with soft reflections, serene and sacred, cool silver-blue light with warm stone tones, deep shadows, anamorphic,
fine grain. No people, nothing on the ledge, no text, no watermark.
```
